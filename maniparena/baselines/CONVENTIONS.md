# Baseline and loader conventions (ManipArena)

Assembled from the per-component rows `maniparena/baselines/conventions_<key>.md` and `maniparena/data/loaders/conventions_<key>.md`.
Score-to-bar mapping for event-level methods: the score of a bar is the score of its causal windows (see each row).
Each paper's default configuration is the benchmark row; informative variants are reported separately (e.g. B4 `area="both"`).
