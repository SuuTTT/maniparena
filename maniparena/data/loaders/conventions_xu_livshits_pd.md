Notes
- JSON keys (clone of 2026-10-03, 435 records): currency, exchange, channelTitle, channelLink, signalTime, duration, max, priceBeforePump, volume, id, ourBuyPrice, ourBuyTime, ourProfit, theoreticalBuyPrice, theoreticalBuyTime, theoreticalProfit, marketHistoryEntries[{rate, amount, timeStamp}]. Exchanges: Cryptopia 216, Yobit 121, Binance 71, Bittrex 27. The build spec quotes 412 pumps (the paper's count after the authors' own filtering).
- 71 Binance records → 69 events after merging announcements of one pair within 60 s. With embedded ticks 56 of them carry a label; 13 records have no tick at or after the signal and are rejected.
- Timezone: ISO-8601 strings with a `Z` suffix → UTC.
- Choices not fixed by the spec: quote currency BTC (not a field of the file); `duration` is kept as `meta["duration_raw"]` and not used for the label because its unit is undocumented (t1 = t0 + 300 s as in lamorgia_pd); records dated before 2017 (placeholder dates) are dropped; exact duplicate ticks are dropped as in the authors' R script.
